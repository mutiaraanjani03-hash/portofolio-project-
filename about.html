About Me

Hello! I'm Mutiara Anjani, an Informatics Engineering student at Sriwijaya University. When I'm not buried in code and coursework, you'll usually find me designing. Graphic design is what I'm exploring most right now, and I'm excited to start learning web development next, so I can turn my designs into real websites that look good and work well.

Outside of class, I'm involved in a few organizations. At GDGoC Unsri, I work on the Creative Design division, and at HMIF FASILKOM UNSRI, I'm part of the Communication and Information (KOMINFO) division. Being there lets me put my design skills into real projects and learn from people who love tech as much as I do.
